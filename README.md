## What’s This Mod All About?

A delightful Forge (and Fabric) mod that squeezes the goodness out of your favorite fruits. Just Enough Juices adds a savory lineup of juices—from Apple to Sweet Berry—with boosted versions that grant juicy perks like **Regeneration**, **Absorption**, and more!

---

## Juicy Features

- Adds a variety of vanilla fruit juices and their “boosted” counterparts:
  - Apple, Carrot, Dried Kelp, Baked Potato, Melon, Pumpkin, Sweet Berry
  - Plus glistering, golden apple, golden carrot juices... and more!
- Boosted juices? They’re basically fruit on performance-enhancing steroids—feel that power!
- Includes *Emerald Dust*—a sparkly ingredient to elevate your juice game.
- Forge & Fabric compatible across multiple Minecraft versions (1.17 → 1.19).

---

## Installation Instructions

1. Ensure you're running **Minecraft Java Edition** with Forge or Fabric installed.
2. Drop the mod `.jar` into your `mods/` folder.
3. Boot up Minecraft using the appropriate modloader.
4. Crack open that juice—and the health boosts!
